# The Implementation Gap

Many companies have built prototypes with LLMs, but few have successfully integrated them into production-ready products. The challenge lies in managing **latency**, **reliability**, and **cost** at scale.

## Lessons from the Field

- **Streaming is Mandatory**: Users expect immediate feedback. Implementing Vercel's AI SDK for streaming responses is critical for a good user experience.
- **Prompt Engineering as Code**: Version and test your prompts just like you do with your logic.
- **Fallback Mechanisms**: What happens when the model generates nonsensical output? Always have a deterministic fallback.

### The Future is Agentic

We are moving away from simple "input-output" patterns toward **agentic workflows** where AI can perform multi-step tasks autonomously. This requires a new mental model for software architecture—one that is built for non-deterministic behavior.

The potential is limitless, but the path to production requires a rigorous, engineering-first approach.
