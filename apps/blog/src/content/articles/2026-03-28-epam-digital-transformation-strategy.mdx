# Beyond the Buzzword

Digital transformation has become one of the most overused terms in technology. Yet for organizations still running critical workloads on legacy infrastructure, the need for modernization is very real. EPAM Systems has positioned itself as a key partner in this journey, bringing a blend of **engineering depth** and **strategic consulting** that few competitors can match.

## The Platform Engineering Approach

Rather than treating transformation as a series of disconnected projects, EPAM advocates for a **platform-first mindset**. This means building internal developer platforms (IDPs) that abstract away infrastructure complexity and empower product teams to ship independently.

### Key Pillars

1. **Cloud-Native Foundations**: Migrating monoliths to microservices architectures on AWS, Azure, or GCP.
2. **Data Mesh Adoption**: Decentralizing data ownership while maintaining governance and discoverability.
3. **DevOps Maturity**: Moving from CI/CD pipelines to full GitOps workflows with policy-as-code.

The results speak for themselves: EPAM's enterprise clients report an average 35% reduction in time-to-market after completing their first transformation phase.
