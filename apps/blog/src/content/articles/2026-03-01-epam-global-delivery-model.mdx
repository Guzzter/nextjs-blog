# Distributed by Design

With over 55,000 engineers spread across more than 50 countries, EPAM's delivery model is one of the most sophisticated in the industry. But scale alone is not the differentiator — it is the **operational framework** that makes distributed delivery predictable and high-quality.

## The Three Pillars

EPAM's global delivery rests on three pillars that work in concert:

1. **Time-Zone Orchestration**: Projects are structured so that handoffs between regions are seamless. A team in Minsk can pick up exactly where a team in Buenos Aires left off.
2. **Standardized Practices**: From code review protocols to incident response runbooks, every team follows the same playbook regardless of geography.
3. **Cultural Integration**: EPAM invests heavily in cross-cultural training and communication workshops to eliminate friction between distributed team members.

### The Follow-the-Sun Model

For mission-critical engagements, EPAM operates a **follow-the-sun** support model where issues are continuously triaged across time zones, ensuring that no incident waits for a single team to wake up.

This model has enabled EPAM to maintain a 99.2% SLA compliance rate across its top 100 enterprise accounts.
