# The Migration Paradox

Most organizations underestimate cloud migration. They assume it is a technology problem when, in reality, it is an **organizational change problem**. EPAM has completed over 300 large-scale migrations, and the lessons learned are remarkably consistent.

## The Three Migration Anti-Patterns

EPAM's experience has revealed three common anti-patterns that derail cloud initiatives:

1. **Lift-and-Shift Everything**: Moving VMs to the cloud without rearchitecting leads to higher costs and no performance gains.
2. **Big Bang Cutover**: Attempting to migrate everything at once introduces unacceptable risk and organizational fatigue.
3. **Ignoring the People**: Technical migration without training and cultural change results in shadow IT and cloud sprawl.

### The Strangler Fig Pattern

EPAM's preferred approach for complex legacy systems is the **Strangler Fig Pattern**. New features are built as cloud-native microservices while the legacy system is gradually decomposed. This allows teams to deliver value continuously while reducing technical debt incrementally.

The key insight: migration is not a project with an end date. It is a continuous process of modernization and optimization.
