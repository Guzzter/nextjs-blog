# The Design System Paradox

A design system that is too rigid stifles innovation. One that is too flexible creates inconsistency. EPAM has spent years refining its approach to building design systems that strike the right balance, serving organizations with 50+ product teams and thousands of components.

## Federated Governance

EPAM advocates for a **federated model** where a core team maintains foundational tokens, primitives, and guidelines, while product teams contribute domain-specific components back to the system.

### The Technology Layer

- **Design Tokens**: Platform-agnostic variables (color, spacing, typography) that compile to CSS, iOS, and Android targets.
- **Component Libraries**: Built in React with full accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA) and comprehensive Storybook documentation.
- **Figma Integration**: Two-way sync between Figma components and code ensures designers and developers always work from the same source of truth.

### Measuring Success

EPAM tracks design system adoption through three metrics: **component coverage** (percentage of UI built with system components), **contribution rate** (components contributed by product teams), and **time-to-first-screen** (how quickly a new product team can ship their first page using the system).
