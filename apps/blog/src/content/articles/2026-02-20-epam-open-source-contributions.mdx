# Why Open Source Matters at EPAM

For a services company, open-source contributions might seem counterintuitive. Why give away intellectual property? EPAM's leadership sees it differently: open source is the **talent magnet** and **innovation engine** that keeps the company competitive.

## Notable Projects

EPAM has released and actively maintains several open-source projects that have gained traction in the developer community:

- **ReportPortal**: An AI-powered test automation dashboard used by thousands of QA teams worldwide.
- **UUI (Universal UI)**: A comprehensive React component library built for enterprise-grade applications.
- **Eco System**: A suite of tools for managing large-scale content operations across multiple CMS platforms.

### The Internal-to-External Pipeline

EPAM follows a deliberate process for open-sourcing internal tools. A project must prove its value across at least three internal engagements before it is considered for public release. This ensures that only mature, well-documented tools reach the community.

### Impact on Hiring

Engineers who contribute to open source are 2.5x more likely to accept an EPAM offer, according to internal recruiting data. The ability to point to public, impactful work is a powerful draw for top talent.
